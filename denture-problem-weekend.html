Full and partial dentures

Your denture cracked, loosened or started rubbing

Bring the denture and every fragment. Avoid home glue, hot water and bending clasps, because a repair attempt can make professional treatment harder.

Stop if wearing it causes injury

A sharp edge, unstable denture or painful pressure spot can irritate tissue. If wearing the appliance causes a sore or makes chewing unsafe, remove it and store it in a clean protective container until it can be evaluated.

Save every part

Bring the denture even if it appears beyond repair. Save broken teeth, acrylic pieces and clasps. Do not place a partial denture in a tissue or pocket where it can be lost or bent. Avoid boiling or very hot water, which can distort some denture materials.

Repair, reline or replacement?

Repair

May address a crack, separated tooth or certain fractures when the pieces and fit are suitable.

Reline

Changes the tissue-fitting surface when the denture is intact but no longer adapts well.

Replacement

May be more predictable when the denture is extensively damaged, worn or poorly fitting.

An examination helps distinguish a denture problem from changes in the gums, bone or remaining teeth. Laboratory time may be required, so an evaluation does not guarantee same-day completion.

Bring this information

  • Age of the denture and whether it is full or partial
  • When the fit or damage changed
  • All pieces and any previous repair history
  • Location of sore spots or chewing difficulty
  • Insurance or Medicaid information
