Prepare for a Colorado Medicaid dental visit

A few details can make eligibility checks and appointment planning smoother, especially for a first visit or a recent change in coverage.

Have this information ready

  • Patient’s full legal name and date of birth
  • Current Health First Colorado member number or state ID when available
  • Photo identification and a working contact number
  • Current medication list and important health conditions or allergies
  • Recent dental records or X-rays, if another office can provide them
  • A clear description of the reason for the visit

Eligibility and treatment are separate steps

An active eligibility check confirms enrollment for a particular date. It does not diagnose the dental problem or determine that every recommended service is payable. Some care may involve clinical criteria, frequency limits, records or prior authorization. After an examination, the dental team can explain the treatment recommendation and what information may be needed.

Ask the office

  • Is my eligibility showing as active for the appointment date?
  • Should I request records from a previous dentist?
  • Will the first visit be an examination, treatment visit or both?
  • What should I bring for a child or another family member?
