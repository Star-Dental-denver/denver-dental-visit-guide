Appointment preparation

What should you bring to a dental appointment?

The right information can reduce delays and help the team understand your health, benefits and immediate concern.

For every new patient

  • Photo identification
  • Insurance or Medicaid member information
  • Current medication list, including nonprescription medicines
  • Allergies, medical conditions and recent surgeries
  • Previous dentist and available records or X-rays
  • Questions and the main reason for the visit

For an urgent problem

Bring any lost crown, broken filling, tooth fragment, denture part or appliance. Write down when symptoms or injury began, how they have changed, and what triggers pain. Share fever, swelling, bleeding or medication taken for the problem.

For a child

Bring the child’s health and medication information, insurance details, and the name of a previous dental office. Tell the team about sensory or communication needs, prior experiences, fears and what helps the child feel comfortable.

For a denture appointment

Bring the current denture even if it is broken, along with every piece. Explain where it rubs, whether it moves while eating, how old it is and whether it has been repaired or relined before.

For Health First Colorado

Have the member’s full legal name, date of birth and current member number or state ID when available. Active eligibility is distinct from clinical coverage for a particular service; an examination and records may be needed before the team can explain the next steps.

Do not delay urgent medical help to gather paperwork when there is difficulty breathing or swallowing, major trauma, rapidly spreading swelling or uncontrolled bleeding.
